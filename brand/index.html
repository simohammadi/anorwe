01 · The name

One who carries the sun.

Anorwë
Anor — "sun" in Sindarin, Tolkien's Elvish. The light that rises in the east.
-wë — an Elvish name-ending, as in Finwë and Ingwë. It reads as a person, not a thing.

anorwe is the one who carries the sun. That's our role: we step into the dark corners of a project, where the data is messy, the ROI is unclear and nobody trusts the prototype yet. We make them visible, then give them energy.
